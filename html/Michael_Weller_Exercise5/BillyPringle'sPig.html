Now have you ever heard
Of Billy Pringle's Pig?
It was not very small,
Nor yet so very big:
When alive it lived in clover,
Now its dead and that's all over;
Poor Billy, he lay down and died,
And Betsy, she sat up and cried:
So there's an end of three;
Of Billy Pringle, he,
Of Betsy Pringle, she,
And poor piggy wiggee.
