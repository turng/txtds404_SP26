LOCAL RELEASES

SUBHUMANS

Death to the Sickoids/Oh Canaduh

Crass Productions no label

I like these guys. The A-side is good
(although I confess I'm not really
sure what Sickoids are), with nice
harmonies. The vocals are good, it's
hard to find a good male voice in Canaduh.
I think this band has potential
not fully realized
While the mix on side two is lousy,
the pace is fast and the music is strong.
Nice drumming and guitar work,
but where is the bass? I think this
deserves to be the A-side. It's a great
song which could make an anthem
for stepped-on Canadians. I can hear
Margaret Trudeau humming it now......
"Don't have a job/Can't pay the rent/
And now we're paying interest on the
money we've been lent........."
