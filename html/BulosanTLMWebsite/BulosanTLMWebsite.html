The Lonesome Mermaid

Carlos Bulosan

Every once in a while the mermaid emerged from the depths of the river
when the moon was full and claimed her victims. She would appear among us
in the person of a beautiful young woman, then suddenly disappear with her
new young man. We often wondered why she always took with her the most
promising man in our town. We burned candles and chanted prayers when we
knew that the time had come when she would appear again.
