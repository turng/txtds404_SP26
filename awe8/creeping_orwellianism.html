"Creeping Orwellianism" is a political prose piece/rant about invasive drug testing policies. The name "Ol Gory" is a play on words for the term "Old Glory," as in the flag of the United States. The imagery portrays the staining of the flag through the wrongdoings of the American Government. In many radical eyes at the time, America was no longer the leader of the "free world," but rather a "fanatically capitalist power, willing to follow militarist policies of nuclear intimidation and conventional military intervention to protect its politico-economic interests".
